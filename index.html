Task rollouts

A success and a failure from each task.

rearrange_blocks

The button is pressed before the block reaches the empty mat

The button is pressed at step 203, but the trajectory shows that the block never reaches the target mat. The episode ends at the 700-step limit without completing the task.

blocks_ranking_try

The episode times out before completing the ordering task

This episode reaches the 3,500-step limit. The available records do not yet identify the first failed action, so they do not establish whether the problem is the chosen order or an incomplete transfer.

put_back_block

The button is pressed before the block is centered

The evaluation record explicitly flags a button press before the block is centered. The task remains incomplete when the episode reaches its 500-step limit.

swap_blocks

Two transfers work; the third grasp misses

The third grasp is 2.1 cm off. The gripper contacts the block’s edge, pushes it, and then lifts empty. It still moves to the destination tray, opens, and presses the button, leaving the third transfer incomplete.

battery_try

The second battery is placed off target and slips away

The second battery is lowered about 3 cm off target, toward the first battery’s position. It slips during the descent and falls onto the table. The policy keeps adjusting the first battery without retrieving the second.

swap_T

The blocks change sides, but the second T has the wrong angle

When the second T is placed, its orientation is about 17° from the target, beyond the 15° tolerance. Further adjustments also move the first T away from its required orientation, leaving the task incomplete.

cover_blocks

Covering works; the first uncovering step is wrong

This episode requires the same uncovering order as the successful example above, but the policy removes the middle lid first. The order violation is recorded at step 637 and permanently fails the task; removing the remaining lids cannot undo it.

observe_and_pickup

The target is a mouse; the policy lifts a stapler

The task shows a mouse at the start and then hides it behind a wall. The policy does grasp and lift an object, but it chooses the stapler about 7 cm from the mouse.

press_button

The card says one press; the policy makes four

The button whose card shows 1 is pressed four times, so confirmation is rejected. Each physical press registers exactly once, ruling out duplicate triggers. The records do not yet distinguish misreading the card, forgetting its value, or miscounting presses.

place_block_mat

The first reach goes to an empty mat, followed by an empty transfer

At step 60, the gripper closes over an empty mat, 9.5 cm from the leftmost block, without making contact. It then lifts, moves to the green mat, opens, and performs the return motion without carrying the block.
